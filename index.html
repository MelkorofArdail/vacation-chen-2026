DRIVE2:45to hotel

Sat · Aug 15…

Kaiserburgbahn Cable Car + Kaiserburg Bob

WALK10min to valley station

Ride the Kaiserburgbahn cable car up over 2,000m for sweeping Nockberge views, then head back down to the valley station for the Kaiserburg Bob — Carinthia's first roller-bobsleigh, a 1.4km summer toboggan track with 26 curves and a 7m loop at the finish. Great for kids 6+; Aviv rides tandem with a parent.

  • Kaiserburg Bob: ~€8–14 per rider depending on rides bought
  • Aviv (5) rides tandem in the same bob as an adult
  • Cable car: ~09:00–16:00, weather-dependent at the top
  • Bring a light jacket — it's noticeably cooler at 2,000m

Brunnach Biosphere Park + St. Kathrein Spa

DRIVE15min (Brunnach)

Morning at the Brunnach Biosphere Park activity park — playground, a raft pond, and a barrier-free hiking trail with big Nockberge mountain views, all inside Austria's largest UNESCO biosphere reserve. Afternoon: back down to the village for the St. Kathrein thermal spa — a relaxed, in-village half-day to soak and unwind.

  • Brunnach Park: mostly free, small entry for some activities
  • St. Kathrein thermal spa: ~€15–20 adult, family rates available
  • Raft pond + barrier-free trail: easy for all ages including Aviv

Pletna Boat to Bled Island + Bled Castle

DRIVE5min to dock

Morning: take a traditional pletna flat-boat to Bled Island — the iconic church visit, climb the 99 steps, ring the wishing bell (every family member gets a wish!). Afternoon: hike or drive up to Bled Castle for the best aerial view of the lake + museum visit.

  • Pletna boat: ~€20 adult / €10 child (4–12) / Aviv ~€10 — cash only on boat — family ~€70
  • Bled Castle: ~€15 adult / ~€6 child (6–15) / Aviv free — family ~€42
  • ⚠️ Bring cash for the pletna — the boatmen don't take cards
  • Castle printing press demo: kids love it, included in entry
If it rains: Skip the boat — the castle is covered and the views are dramatic in moody weather.

Lake Bohinj + Vogel Cable Car

DRIVE30min west

Quieter, wilder Lake Bohinj inside Triglav National Park — turquoise glacier water, mountain backdrop, and you can wild-swim right from the shore. Then take the Vogel cable car to 1535m altitude for big, sweeping Julian Alps panoramas — the best mountain views of the whole trip.

  • Vogel cable car: ~€33 adult / €16 child (6–14) / Aviv ~€8 — family ~€90
  • Lake Bohinj swimming: free — beach area near the boat dock
  • Pack warm layer for the cable car (mountain wind at 1535m)
If cable car closed (weather): Walk the Bohinj lakeside trail (3km easy loop) and swim all day instead.
